Visuals

Where to find the teaser, FB Live thumbnail and announcements fast. All official designs are in one shared Canva folder.

How our Canva works

  1. Our official designs live in John's Canva team. Everyone in the team can open and edit them. The JIA Media account (jiamedia.alabang@gmail.com) is in it too.
  2. Sign in to Canva with the account that's in the team, then open the shared folder JIA Alabang - Media Publication Material ↗.
  3. Use the designs marked (Official). A design marked (Archive) is old; don't post from it.
  4. Each design holds many pages, one per week or month. Download as PNG.

Not in the team yet, or a link won't open? Message John or James in the Visual Team GC.
