Next up

ASSESS chip vote

Design agreed · not built yet

A quick, private way for people to tell us how the service went. One question each Sunday, three jars, one chip each.

How it works

  1. Take 1 chip from the container beside the station.
  2. Step behind the screen, read this week's question, and drop the chip in 1 of the 3 jars.
  3. That's it. Nobody watches the station, and the jars hide how others voted.

The plan

  • Place: the white wall under the stairs, ground floor, beside the hall's glass doors.
  • 3 opaque jars with small slots, inside a tri-fold privacy screen on a small table.
  • The question changes weekly (for example audio, lyrics, welcome). The 3 answers are printed with it.
  • After service, anyone can count: one jar at a time, in piles of 10, then write it down.
  • The QR form stays for anyone who wants to say more.
  • Trial for about 4 Sundays first. If few people vote, we fix the spot or the question before anything else.

Second livestream camera (DJI Pocket 4)

Exploring · not decided

Right now the X-T3 is our only livestream camera, and it sits in the center aisle where it blocks people. A second angle would fix that.

  • Leading idea for a roaming shot: DJI Pocket 4 (camera with built-in gimbal) with DJI SDR Transmission 2 to send video to the livestream laptop without cables.
  • Why: quick to set up, steady handheld shots, easy for volunteers, and the X-T4 stays free for photos.
  • Other options still on the table: using the X-T4 as a fixed second angle (no cost), or a fixed PTZ camera.
  • Still to decide: do we need a fixed side view or a moving shot? Nothing is bought yet.
